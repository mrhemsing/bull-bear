# Canonical Prompt Pack

This prompt pack defines the 20 still-image production prompts for the canonical Bull Bear state library.

## Base prompt spine

Use this spine for every state:

```text
colossal cinematic market beast portrait, the same recurring hybrid bull-bear titan, photorealistic dark fantasy realism, Wall Street financial district destruction, smoke, dust, debris, scattered paper money, low-angle hero composition, dramatic blockbuster lighting, high-contrast atmosphere, ultra-detailed fur, horns, skull, claws, hoof structure, same visual DNA, same centered hero framing, one believable species, no text, no logo, no collage
```

## State prompts

### State 01 — Extremely Bearish
The creature is at maximum bearish dominance: enormous claws, massive bear jaw, broad skull, thick shadow-heavy fur, low crushing predator posture, cold blue-black atmosphere, blazing crimson eyes, overwhelming menace, only faint residual bull traits.

### State 02 — Very Strong Bearish
Very strong bearish dominance: huge claws, heavy bear skull, looming posture, dense fur, red eyes, cold finance-apocalypse atmosphere, bull traits still present but suppressed.

### State 03 — Strong Bearish
Strong bearish dominance: clear bear morphology, heavier claws and forelimbs, red eyes, lowered stalking posture, colder shadow-rich environment, meaningful but reduced bull presence.

### State 04 — Firmly Bearish
Firmly bearish: bearish mass and jaw dominate, claws prominent, horns smaller and less emphasized, red eyes, dark atmospheric pressure, restrained but dangerous forward tension.

### State 05 — Bearish
Bear-dominant hybrid: broad jaw, claw-led forelimbs, denser fur, cooler atmosphere, red eye glow, horns remain visible but secondary.

### State 06 — Moderately Bearish
Moderately bearish: bear side still leads, claws and fur heavier than hoofs and horn power, red eyes softer, atmosphere still cold but less extreme.

### State 07 — Lean Bearish
Lean bearish: slightly more bear than bull, visible claws and heavier jawline, subdued red glow, balanced but shadow-biased market beast.

### State 08 — Slightly Bearish
Slightly bearish: small bear edge in fur mass, claws, and posture, restrained crimson eyes, hybrid anatomy remains highly visible.

### State 09 — Mildly Bearish
Mildly bearish: nearly balanced hybrid with a light bear tilt, subtle claw emphasis, gentle red eye glow, minimal cold shadow influence.

### State 10 — Neutral
Perfectly balanced 50-50 bull-bear hybrid, equal bull and bear anatomy, believable integration of hoof and claw structure, amber-white eyes, controlled cinematic menace, no side dominates.

### State 11 — Mildly Bullish
Mildly bullish: nearly balanced hybrid with a light bull tilt, subtle horn growth, slight hoof emphasis, gentle green eye glow, minimal warm uplift in the atmosphere.

### State 12 — Slightly Bullish
Slightly bullish: small bull edge in horn power, shoulder strength, and hoof structure, restrained emerald eyes, hybrid anatomy remains highly visible.

### State 13 — Lean Bullish
Lean bullish: slightly more bull than bear, visible horn emphasis, stronger chest, green eye glow, balanced but heat-biased market beast.

### State 14 — Moderately Bullish
Moderately bullish: bull side leads, horn size and shoulder structure stronger than claws and fur mass, green eyes, warmer atmosphere, forward tension increasing.

### State 15 — Bullish
Bull-dominant hybrid: clear horn power, more muscular shoulders, hoof impact emphasis, brighter green eye glow, the bear side still present but secondary.

### State 16 — Firmly Bullish
Firmly bullish: bullish chest and horn dominance, hoof-led lower limbs, hotter atmosphere, green eyes, restrained but powerful charge energy.

### State 17 — Strong Bullish
Strong bullish dominance: clear bull morphology, larger horns, stronger chest and shoulders, green eyes, heated atmosphere, meaningful but reduced bear presence.

### State 18 — Very Strong Bullish
Very strong bullish dominance: massive horns, dominant shoulder and chest power, hoof impact force, intense emerald eyes, rising heat and market-fire energy, bear traits suppressed.

### State 19 — Exceptionally Bullish
Exceptionally bullish: colossal horn structure, explosive upward force, strong hoof emphasis, vivid green eyes, glowing hot finance-apocalypse atmosphere, only faint residual bear traits.

### State 20 — Extremely Bullish
The creature is at maximum bullish dominance: gigantic horns, maximum chest and shoulder power, heavy hoof impact, blazing emerald eyes, heated gold-ember atmosphere, unstoppable upward market fury, only the slightest trace of bear ancestry.

## Production note

Generate the 20 stills as a full ordered set and review them side-by-side. The most important question is whether adjacent states feel like the same creature shifting by one readable degree.
